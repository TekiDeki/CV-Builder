Your file

The CV is saved in this browser as you type. Export a copy to keep it, or to move it to another computer.

In the print dialog pick "Save as PDF", paper A4 or Letter, margins none, and keep the scale at 100 percent.

Why it is free all the way to the download

No paywall at the end

The category has one loud complaint: build the CV for free, then find the PDF behind a card. Here the print button is the whole product, and it costs nothing.

No watermark, no account

Nothing is stamped on your page, nothing is asked for your address, and no email is collected. There is no backend to collect it.

Text, not a picture

The classic layout is one column of real text with real headings, so the machine that reads applications can read it too.
